Site Name: Web Elevate

The name "Web Elevate" clearly communicates the idea of elevating your digital presence through website solutions. It represents progress, professionalism, and online growth.

Suggested domain: webelevate.pro (optional)

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to offer web design and development services at three different levels (Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3), providing tailored solutions for startups, medium-sized businesses, and advanced custom projects.

Scenarios

  • What is the difference between a Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 website?
  • How much does a basic website cost and how long does it take to build?
  • Where can I see examples of previous work or templates?

Color Scheme

  • Professional Blue (#0066cc): Will be used for headings, primary buttons, and links.
  • Soft Gray (#f0f4f8): Used as a general background color to give a clean look.
  • Black (#2c2c2c): For body text, ensuring good readability.

Typography

  • Montserrat: For titles, headings, and buttons. Conveys modernity and clarity.
  • Roboto: For body text and descriptive sections. Highly readable and professional.
